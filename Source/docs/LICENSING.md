# Licensing and forks

The authoritative license is the root LICENSE file: unmodified MIT terms with the
project copyright notice. Keep that notice and permission text with redistributed
copies or substantial portions. MIT permits modification, redistribution and
commercial use; it does not require forks to use the same design or publish changes.

Maintainers welcome additions that preserve the primary functions and blue compact
interface. Those requirements govern upstream merge decisions only. Adding them as
binding restrictions to MIT would contradict the chosen permissive licensing intent.

## Before a public release

- [ ] Confirm copyright attribution and contributors' permission for all code/assets.
- [ ] Retain LICENSE in source, portable and installer/MSIX payloads.
- [ ] Check every new dependency's license and redistribution requirements.
- [ ] Update THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md when distributing third-party material.
- [ ] Do not ship private signing keys, vendor installers or meeting content.

No separate NOTICE file or CLA is required by this project's MIT license itself.
Dependency licenses may impose their own notice requirements. The privacy and
security documents describe behaviour; they are not additional license conditions.
Product/vendor names identify compatibility only and do not imply endorsement.
