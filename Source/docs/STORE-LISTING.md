## English (United States) (en-US)

### Description

Control a compatible Windows camera during meetings, recordings, and live streams. Pan, tilt, and zoom to frame a shot. Save views of the speaker, the stage, or a demo table and recall them with one click.

Use Camera Control Studio alongside your meeting or streaming app. Keep separate profiles for different setups. Adjust image settings or field of view where the camera driver supports them.

The app has been tested with a range of webcams, including models similar to the Logitech Brio.
